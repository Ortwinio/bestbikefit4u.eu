# S1 batch interface

`convex/emails/transitionBatchSchema.ts` exports `billingTransitionRuns`; the schema owner imports and registers that table. No public endpoints are added.

`emails/billingBatches:runDaily` is internal and runs daily at 00:30 UTC. It scans at most 50 entitlement records, then schedules its next cursor with the original scan timestamp. Both billing flags must be enabled. The renewal constant defaults to 30 days. Revoked and appointment-only entitlements are excluded. Cancelled periods do not get renewal reminders, but do get expiry notices once access ends. Renewed periods represent paid renewal periods and remain eligible for future renewal reminders and expiry notices. Expiry suppresses an active replacement annual entitlement or replacement single entitlement for the same bike. The queue and delivery layer recheck current eligibility and persist deduplication.

`emails/transitionBatches:begin` takes `kind` (`transition_announcement` or `transition_reminder`), `launchAt` (positive safe integer milliseconds), optional `dryRun` (defaults true), and optional `dryRunId`. Call `continueBatch({runId})` until `complete` is true. Each call processes at most 25 users. Both operations require the authenticated `billing_admin` role (or super-admin). Responses contain only opaque `runId` and `scanned`, `eligible`, `queued`, `complete`; no recipient records.

Applying requires a completed dry run from the same admin, kind and launch date, completed within 24 hours. Evidence is single-use. Applied runs must finish within 24 hours of their creation; stale runs need a fresh dry run. Billing must remain enabled for reminder apply and every reminder continuation. Dry runs may run with billing disabled and never enqueue mail.

The explicitly admin-authorized transition announcement is a prelaunch service communication, not a Stripe payment path. It may therefore apply with billing disabled. Its queue job carries `transitionRunId`; the queue/sender verify the applied run and its matching completed admin-bound dry-run evidence. No other mail kind bypasses the billing flags. This exception enables the required announcement fourteen days before launch while payment flags remain disabled.

Both campaigns require at least one report. Reminder campaigns additionally require an unredeemed offer with matching launch date, whose deadline is greater than now and at most seven days away. Marketing consent is deliberately not a selection criterion. Delivery owns locale/service preferences and send-time eligibility.

Queue contract: `queueBillingEmail` returns true only when a new durable message was queued; false for duplicates/suppression. Stable keys are `renewal:<subscription>:<periodStart>`, `expired:<entitlement>`, `transition_announcement:<launchAt>:<user>` and `transition_reminder:<offer>:<user>`. No actual identifiers appear in this artifact.

Validation: 19 focused behavioral tests pass. They exercise renewal (including paid second-year periods), expiry (including ended cancelled and renewed periods), revoked/refund exclusion, superseding access by bike, repeat dedupe keys, scan limits and scheduler continuation, flags off, report targeting independent of marketing, dry-run-first requirements, admin/type/date matching, stale/single-use evidence, aggregate-only output, reminder window and apply kill switch. The prelaunch exception test proves authorization and dry-run evidence remain required with billing off and that the queue receives the applied run proof. Sender tests own rejecting forged or missing proof. Scoped ESLint passed. Full integration gates are owned by S1 lead.
