# S1 delivery component

Persisted billing outbox, transactional send-key deduplication, atomic claim leases, bounded retry, stable provider idempotency, latest locale and service preferences, invoice-payment linkage and refund rechecks. Seven sender kinds implemented. Personal annual welcome and standalone receipt use validated HTTPS agenda URLs. Separate truthful NL/EN transition reminder states the actual redemption deadline.

Renewed means a paid renewal period, not superseded: renewal and expiry remain eligible. Expiry suppresses replacement access; cancelled first-year access can still receive expiry. Immediate renewed cancellation awaits refund evidence, including delayed payment linkage. Full refunds suppress purchase/welcome/reminder/expiry, but cancellation includes the refund amount. Missing config or evidence never produces a sent record.

Validation: 35 delivery tests and 19 batch tests passed together, including fail-closed purchase/subscription product mismatch tests. Convex standalone TypeScript, focused ESLint and lint:prices passed. The sender shares the batch renewal-window constant. Provider mocked throughout; no real mail, Stripe, env changes or deploys.

Recovery limits: up to six delivery attempts within 20 hours from first real claim; five-minute leases recover crashed actions. Missing payment or exact cancellation refund evidence consumes no attempts; verified invoice-payment/refund events wake pending jobs, including evidence delayed by days. Terminal failed jobs retain generic error codes for operator inspection. Retry provider key and provider request are stable across attempts. The request is retained in the database while delivery is unresolved and removed after successful delivery; changed recipient fails closed. A delivery success followed by failed persistence reuses the identical request within the dedupe window. A refund arriving after provider acceptance cannot retract mail. No delivery payload or provider error text is logged.
