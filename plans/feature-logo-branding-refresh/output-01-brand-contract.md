## Palette Roles

- primary interaction blue: `#089BE9`
- supporting blue depth: `#0587DC`
- accent/warning orange: `#F68D17`
- brand charcoal: `#1C1F29`
- supporting slate: `#4E6778`
- light neutral: `#DEDEDF`

## Surface Decisions

- keep blue as the main CTA, focus, link, and active-state color
- use orange as selective emphasis through accent and warning surfaces
- use slate/charcoal for structure, borders, panels, and foreground depth
- use the full logo on roomy surfaces and the app icon on compact mobile dashboard surfaces
