## Objective

Turn the homepage into a clearer acquisition page that shows first value early, makes calculators prominent, and routes new users into a low-friction next step.

## Tasks

1. Replace the hero primary CTA so it opens the bike-fit calculator or another public intake step instead of `/login`.
2. Rework the hero supporting copy so it sells concrete rider outcomes before account creation.
3. Add a compact trust strip near the hero with methodology, practical outputs, and transparent limitations.
4. Move calculators much higher on the page:
   feature one primary calculator and expose the calculator group earlier than long educational sections.
5. Ensure the quick-check and calculator sections clearly show the next step after first value.

## Deliverable

An updated homepage with a value-first funnel, earlier calculator visibility, clearer trust framing, and cleaner CTA hierarchy.
