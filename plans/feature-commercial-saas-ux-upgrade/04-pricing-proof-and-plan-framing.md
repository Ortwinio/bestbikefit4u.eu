# Pricing Proof And Plan Framing

## Objective

Strengthen pricing so it sells outcomes, shows proof, and adds one clear confidence mechanism without inventing unsupported claims.

## Inputs

- [page.tsx](/Users/ortwinverreck/Developer/bestbikefit4u/src/app/(public)/pricing/page.tsx)
- [commercial.ts](/Users/ortwinverreck/Developer/bestbikefit4u/src/config/commercial.ts)
- Output from Step 01

## Tasks

1. Rewrite plan framing so Free and Pro are described by rider use cases and outcomes, not only features.
2. Add a visible proof module near the plan cards:
   sample report preview, named testimonial, or “what your result looks like” block.
3. Add one confidence mechanism grounded in current product reality:
   annual savings, support assurance, live-report example, or another verified option.
4. Improve plan comparison hierarchy so the recommended plan and entry plan are both easy to understand.
5. Tighten CTA copy so users know whether they are starting free, creating an account, or upgrading for deeper outputs.
6. Preserve the rule that pricing only shows live plans and live features.
7. Keep the FAQ section aligned with stronger conversion language instead of purely defensive clarifications.

## Deliverable

A pricing page that is clearer, more persuasive, and better supported by practical proof and confidence cues.

## Completion Checklist

- [ ] Free vs Pro is framed by outcome and use case.
- [ ] At least one proof module is added near the buying decision.
- [ ] At least one confidence mechanism is visible.
- [ ] Live-product honesty is preserved.
- [ ] CTA wording is more explicit about next steps.
