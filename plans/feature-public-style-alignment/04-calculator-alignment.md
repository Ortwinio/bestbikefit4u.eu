# Step 04: Calculator Alignment

## Objective

Bring calculator pages closer to the `My Profile` quality level without harming clarity or speed.

## Target Pages

- bike fit calculator
- saddle height calculator
- frame size calculator
- crank length calculator
- tire pressure calculator
- any closely related public calculator landing pages

## Tasks

1. Standardize calculator layout:
   - hero or intro section
   - framed input area
   - framed result area
   - helper/explanation panels
   - next-step CTA block
2. Use profile-style helper/info boxes to explain inputs and outputs.
3. Use metric-style visual framing for results where appropriate.
4. Keep forms fast and legible; do not add decorative clutter around inputs.

## Acceptance

- Calculators share a common structural pattern.
- Input and output blocks are easier to distinguish visually.
- Helper content is more readable and better framed.
- Calculator pages feel consistent with the rest of the updated public site.
