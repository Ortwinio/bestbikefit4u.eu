# Step 05: Text Pages And Illustrations

## Objective

Improve text-heavy public pages with better section framing and selective illustration support.

## Candidate Pages

- guide index and guide detail pages
- use-case index and detail pages
- science pages
- measurement guide
- other public pages that are predominantly text

## Tasks

1. Identify text-heavy pages that currently read like long text stacks.
2. Add corresponding illustration support where it improves comprehension or rhythm.
3. Define illustration rules:
   - one illustration per major page or major section when justified
   - illustration must match the topic
   - maintain a consistent visual family
4. If custom illustrations do not yet exist, define placeholder strategy:
   - reusable product illustration blocks
   - consistent icon-led graphic panels
   - future-ready asset slots
5. Use framed section shells to break up dense content.

## Acceptance

- Text-heavy pages gain better scanning structure.
- Illustration use is intentional and consistent.
- Pages with only text no longer feel visually empty when an illustration would materially help.
