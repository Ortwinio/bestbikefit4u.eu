# Step 5: Naming And Routing

## Objective

Define a clean naming and route-consistency plan for the public calculators.

## Tasks

1. Audit current calculator route naming in EN and NL.
2. Identify mixed-language and mixed-pattern routes.
3. Propose a standard route model for:
   - English
   - Dutch
4. Define migration constraints:
   - SEO preservation
   - redirects
   - analytics continuity
   - internal-link updates
5. Define the naming contract for calculator titles and CTA references.

## Output

Create `output-05-naming-routing.md`.

## Success Criteria

- Route conventions are explicit and consistent.
- The plan preserves SEO and analytics while improving polish.
- Calculator naming is no longer mixed or inconsistent across locales.
