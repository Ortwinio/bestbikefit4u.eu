# Step 03: Validation And Rollout

Turn the audit into release-safe implementation work.

## What To Produce

1. A phased remediation plan: critical, structural, cleanup.
2. A ticket-ready backlog with scope, files, acceptance criteria, effort, and risk.
3. A validation checklist covering crawl, canonical, robots, redirects, and headings.
4. Any open questions that require infra or Search Console confirmation.

## Done When

- engineering can implement without rediscovering the route and template behavior
- product can sequence the work by impact and effort
- QA can validate the fixes with deterministic checks
